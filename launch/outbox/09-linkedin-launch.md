My coding agents kept stopping every time I closed my laptop. So I built Otto.

Otto runs your agents on a cloud machine. Close the laptop and they keep going.

When one needs your OK, say to push to git, your phone buzzes and you tap Allow. The jobs you run every night go on a schedule and run at 2 AM while everything's asleep.

No account and no server of mine in the middle. Your machine, your keys.

It's free and open source, on Mac, Windows, Linux and Android, and it's live on Hacker News today.

If you use coding agents, try it and tell me what breaks. Links are in the first comment.
