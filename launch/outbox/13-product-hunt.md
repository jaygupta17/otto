**Description:**

```
Otto runs OpenCode coding agents on a cloud machine. Steer them from a desktop or phone app: approve a git push from your phone, schedule overnight runs, and watch several agents at once. It also runs OpenCode right beside the app on your own machine, so a long job keeps going after you quit.

Free, open source, no account.
```
