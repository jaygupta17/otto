## The two teasers are your cheapest test

Teaser 1 is four lines and an 8.6-second clip, no links, no product name. If it lands flat
on Saturday you still have three days to adjust before the launch post. Teaser 2 lands the
permission moment the night before, with `15-mobile-prompt.png`.

Both post in your waking hours — 19:30 and 20:30 PT are morning for you, and 08:00/09:00 IST
is the middle of the night. Your earliest launch-day item is 20:30 IST.

## Provider emails carry no film on the 15th

An unlisted YouTube link is one forward from being public, and none of the four can act
before the 20th. They get a screenshot that shows their own product in the list. The film
goes out on the 19th with the HN link.

OpenCode maintainers still get the film on the 15th — they're insiders who'll want to see it
before they share it, and they have a reason to.
